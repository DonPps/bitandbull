Privacy Policy — Bit & Bull Studio

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Who uses this app

Bit & Bull Studio is a private tool used only by the owner of the Bit & Bull YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

Data accessed

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app accesses the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API: uploading the channel's videos and thumbnails, reading and replying to comments on the channel, and reading the channel's analytics.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens and analytics reports are stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Through the Bit & Bull YouTube channel's About page.
